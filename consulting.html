Consulting Practice

Selective engagements for people building something that matters.

Kristie consults with municipalities, nonprofits, family businesses, and growing companies who need someone who can sit at any table in the room.

Practice 01

Government & Community Relations

Helping organizations navigate local and state government, build the right coalitions, and turn community input into policy and project outcomes.

Typical engagement: stakeholder mapping, coalition design, public meeting strategy, and ongoing advisory through a key initiative.

Practice 02

Marketing & Brand Strategy

Brand positioning, messaging, and marketing leadership advisory for businesses past startup but not yet at the scale of an in-house CMO. Draws on a Vice President of Marketing tenure at H&H Homes.

Typical engagement: brand audit, positioning workshop, marketing function review, or fractional advisory.

Practice 03

Small Business Operations & Growth

Practical operating advice for owners who are running the business and want a thought partner who has been in the same chair.

Typical engagement: operating review, leadership coaching for the owner, or quarterly advisory through a transition.

Practice 04

Coalition Building & Stakeholder Alignment

When an initiative requires a dozen organizations to row in the same direction, someone has to set the cadence and hold the room. That is the work.

Typical engagement: facilitation, working group design, and a defined runway to a public outcome.
